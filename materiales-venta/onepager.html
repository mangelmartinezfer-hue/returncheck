The problem

Agents are about to buy for us. They can't tell what's actually returnable.

Return policies live in prose, differ by merchant and marketplace seller, and hinge on conditions — the item, the window, the buyer's location. An agent that guesses “yes” when the truth is “final sale” costs its user real money and loses their trust. Today there's no clean, machine-readable source of truth for a specific product.

How it works

Read the policy. Extract with a constrained model. Verify before answering.

STEP 01

Read

Fetches the merchant's published return policy at the edge, in milliseconds.

STEP 02

Extract

A schema-constrained model turns the prose into a structured verdict — it can't emit a value that isn't in the contract.

STEP 03

Verify

The cited clause must appear verbatim on the page and actually support the verdict. If it doesn't, the answer degrades to UNKNOWN.
